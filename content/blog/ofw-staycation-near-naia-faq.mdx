### Is there a good place to stay near NAIA for OFWs coming home?

Parañaque is one area OFWs can consider when looking for accommodation after arriving in Manila. It provides access to Metro Manila and is relatively close to NAIA compared with destinations farther outside the city.

### Is Cozy Spot Azure near NAIA?

Cozy Spot Azure is located at Azure Urban Resort Residences in Bicutan, Parañaque City. It is not located inside the airport, but its Parañaque location can be considered by travelers looking for accommodation with access to NAIA and Metro Manila.

Airport travel times vary depending on traffic, weather, time of day, and which terminal you're using.

Yes, guests can check available dates for multi-night stays. Availability and booking terms depend on the dates selected.

### Is Parañaque a good place to stay after arriving in Manila?

Parañaque can be a practical location for travelers who want access to Metro Manila while remaining relatively close to NAIA. It can also provide convenient access to shopping, dining, family gatherings, and other activities around the city.

### What if my flight arrives late at night?

Check the accommodation's current check-in policy and make sure your expected arrival time can be accommodated. If you have a late-night arrival, confirm the check-in procedure before booking.

### Should I stay near NAIA before an early international flight?

For some travelers, staying closer to the airport the night before an early flight can make the departure less stressful.

However, always allow plenty of time for traffic and airport procedures.
