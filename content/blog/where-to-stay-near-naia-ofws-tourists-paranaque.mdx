### 3. Traffic

Metro Manila traffic can change quickly.

Give yourself extra time.

### 4. Check-In

### 5. Amenities

Think about what you will actually use during your stay.

### 6. Nearby Essentials

### 7. Longer-Stay Terms

If you're staying several nights, confirm the current price, availability, minimum stay, and house rules before booking.
