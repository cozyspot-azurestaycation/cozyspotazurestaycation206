## Can Foreigners Stay in a Private Airbnb or Staycation?

Foreign travelers can use short-term accommodation options, including Airbnb listings, but the specific booking must follow the host's requirements and applicable Philippine entry and accommodation rules.

When arriving in the Philippines, foreign nationals go through immigration inspection.

The Bureau of Immigration's published arrival formalities include requirements such as a valid passport or travel document and proof of eTravel registration, with additional documents potentially required depending on the traveler and visa situation. Its published materials also identify return/onward tickets and proof of accommodation as relevant documents for tourists where applicable.

For your trip, always check the **current official Philippine immigration and visa requirements for your nationality** before traveling.

Your accommodation booking does not replace visa or immigration requirements.

## What About Philippine Visa Requirements?

Visa rules depend on your nationality and circumstances.

The Philippine Bureau of Immigration explains that temporary visitor arrangements can include visa-waiver entry for eligible nationals, temporary visitor visas, and other admission categories.

If you're planning to stay longer, don't assume that an accommodation's availability automatically means you're allowed to remain in the Philippines for that period.

Check the latest information directly with the Philippine Bureau of Immigration or the nearest Philippine embassy or consulate before making long-stay plans.

For example, the Bureau of Immigration provides information about extensions for eligible foreign nationals and temporary visitor arrangements.
