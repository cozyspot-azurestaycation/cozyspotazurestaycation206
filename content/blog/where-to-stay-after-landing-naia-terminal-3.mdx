## Where Is NAIA Terminal 3?

Ninoy Aquino International Airport (NAIA) Terminal 3 is one of the main passenger terminals serving Metro Manila.

If your airline confirmation says NAIA Terminal 3, make sure you plan your accommodation and airport transfer around Terminal 3 specifically.

This is important because NAIA has multiple terminals, and a place that is convenient for Terminal 3 may not be equally convenient for Terminal 1, 2, or 4.

Before booking your accommodation, check your airline confirmation or current flight information so you know which terminal you'll actually use.

## Don't Book Based Only on "Near NAIA"

This is one of the biggest mistakes travelers can make.

A listing may say:

**"Near NAIA."**

But that doesn't automatically mean it's convenient for your particular terminal.

The same applies to:

**"Near Manila Airport."**

Always check:

Which terminal? How far? How do I get there? How long could the trip take during traffic? What happens if I arrive late?

This is especially important because NAIA has multiple terminals spread across the airport area.

A property that is excellent for Terminal 3 may not be the best choice if your next flight departs from Terminal 1 or Terminal 2.
